Negative reinforcement is not punishment

Reinforcement increases a behaviour; punishment aims to decrease it.

The idea

In negative reinforcement, an unpleasant stimulus is removed after an action. Negative describes removal, not a bad outcome.

An example

A seat-belt warning stops when you buckle up, encouraging the action.

Put it into practice

Ask whether the behaviour increases or decreases.
