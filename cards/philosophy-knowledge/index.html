Confidence is not the same as knowledge

Epistemology studies knowledge, justification and belief.

The idea

Feeling certain does not establish that a belief is true or well supported. Philosophers investigate what distinguishes knowledge from a lucky guess.

An example

Guessing tomorrow's weather correctly once does not show that your method was reliable.

Put it into practice

Separate the claim from your reasons for believing it.
