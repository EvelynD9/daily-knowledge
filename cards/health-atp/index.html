Cells need usable energy, not just fuel

ATP helps couple energy-releasing processes to cellular work.

The idea

Cells continually produce and use ATP. It is an energy-transfer molecule rather than a large long-term fuel store.

An example

Muscle contraction uses ATP while metabolism replenishes it from available fuels.

Put it into practice

Distinguish stored fuel from the molecule that helps power immediate work.
