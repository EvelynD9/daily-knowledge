Composition controls the journey of your eye

Composition is the arrangement of visual elements—and it determines how an image unfolds over time.

The idea

Lines, scale, contrast, repetition, and placement create routes for attention. Balance can be symmetrical and stable or asymmetrical and alive.

An example

A diagonal staircase creates movement; a centred figure creates authority; a cropped face can create intimacy or unease.

Put it into practice

Notice where your eye lands first, second, and third. That route is part of the artwork’s meaning.
