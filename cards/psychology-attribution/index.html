Behaviour has a situation behind it

Attribution bias can overemphasise personality and underweight circumstances.

The idea

We often know less about another person's pressures than our own. One action is weak evidence of a permanent trait.

An example

A late colleague seems careless until you learn their train was cancelled.

Put it into practice

Look for a situational explanation before labelling someone.
