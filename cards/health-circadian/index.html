The body keeps time

Circadian rhythms organise many processes on an approximately 24-hour cycle.

The idea

Internal clocks interact with cues such as light. Timing affects patterns of sleepiness and alertness.

An example

Crossing time zones can put the local light-dark cycle out of step with an internal clock.

Put it into practice

Notice both how long and when you sleep.
