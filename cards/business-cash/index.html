Profit is not cash in the bank

Profit and cash flow measure different things.

The idea

A recorded sale may create profit before the customer pays. Cash timing determines whether bills can be paid now.

An example

A profitable business with unpaid invoices can still struggle to cover wages this week.

Put it into practice

Track when money arrives and leaves, not only reported profit.
