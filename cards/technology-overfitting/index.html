Memorising examples is not generalising

Overfitting occurs when a model fits training data but performs poorly on new data.

The idea

A model may learn accidental details rather than useful patterns. Evaluation on separate data helps expose the problem.

An example

A classifier recognises its training photos perfectly but fails on new photographs.

Put it into practice

Judge performance on examples the model did not train on.
