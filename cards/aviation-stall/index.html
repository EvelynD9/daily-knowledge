A stall is an airflow problem

An aerodynamic stall occurs when a wing exceeds its critical angle of attack.

The idea

Flow separation reduces lift capability. A stall is not the same as an engine stopping and is not defined by one universal speed.

An example

Different loading or manoeuvres can change the airspeed at which a wing reaches its critical angle.

Put it into practice

Separate engine condition, airspeed and angle of attack.
