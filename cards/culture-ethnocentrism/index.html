Familiar is not automatically universal

Ethnocentrism judges another culture mainly by one's own cultural standards.

The idea

Treating familiar practices as the only normal ones can block understanding of different meanings.

An example

You assume a different greeting is disrespectful because it is not the greeting you learned.

Put it into practice

Describe the difference before ranking it.
