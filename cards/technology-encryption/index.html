Encryption protects the contents

Encryption transforms readable data into ciphertext using an algorithm and key.

The idea

An authorised recipient uses the appropriate key to recover the message. Protection also depends on implementation and key handling.

An example

An encrypted message can travel across a network without exposing its readable contents to every intermediary.

Put it into practice

Ask who can access the keys, not only whether encryption is mentioned.
