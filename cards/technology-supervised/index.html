Learning can begin with labelled examples

Supervised learning learns relationships between inputs and known target labels.

The idea

Training examples guide a model toward predictions for new inputs. The usefulness of those predictions depends on data and evaluation.

An example

A spam classifier learns from emails labelled spam or not spam.

Put it into practice

Ask how the labels were created and whether new cases resemble the training data.
