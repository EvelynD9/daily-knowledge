Read the document's world, too

Historical context concerns the conditions in which a source was created.

The idea

Audience, institutions, available knowledge and social pressures shape what a document means.

An example

A public speech and a private letter by the same person may use different language for different audiences.

Put it into practice

Ask who could hear or read these words at the time.
