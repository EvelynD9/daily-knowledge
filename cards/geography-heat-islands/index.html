A city can hold extra heat

Urban heat islands are urban areas warmer than nearby surroundings.

The idea

Surfaces, buildings, reduced vegetation and human activity influence heat storage and release. Conditions vary within a city.

An example

A paved area without shade may remain warmer than a nearby tree-covered space.

Put it into practice

Compare land cover when comparing local temperatures.
