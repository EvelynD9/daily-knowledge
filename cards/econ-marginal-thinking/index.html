Big decisions are often made at the margin

Marginal thinking focuses on the benefit and cost of one additional unit.

The idea

The useful question is rarely “Is this good?” It is “Is one more hour, euro, or unit worth more than what it costs me?”

An example

The first hour of preparation may prevent major mistakes. The tenth may only polish details. Their value is not equal.

Put it into practice

When something is already good enough, judge the next increment—not the entire project.
