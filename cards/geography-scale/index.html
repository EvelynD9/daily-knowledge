Scale decides how much fits on a map

A map scale relates distance on the map to distance on the ground.

The idea

At 1:100,000, one unit on the map represents 100,000 of the same units on the ground.

An example

At that scale, 1 centimetre represents 1 kilometre.

Put it into practice

Check scale before estimating distance or expecting detail.
