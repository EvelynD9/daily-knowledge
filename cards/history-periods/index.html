Historical periods are useful boundaries

Periodisation divides the past into named eras for analysis.

The idea

The boundaries highlight some changes and hide others. A transition can happen at different times in different places.

An example

A political revolution may transform government immediately while farming habits change much more slowly.

Put it into practice

Ask what changed at the boundary and for whom.
