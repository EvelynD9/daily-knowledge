The surface is moving beneath us

Plate tectonics describes motion of Earth's lithospheric plates.

The idea

Plates separate, converge or slide past one another. Their boundaries help explain patterns of earthquakes, volcanoes and mountain building.

An example

A band of earthquakes can trace the boundary between moving plates.

Put it into practice

Look at boundaries when interpreting geological patterns.
