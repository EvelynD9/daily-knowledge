One ethical lens looks at outcomes

Consequentialism evaluates actions by their consequences.

The idea

Different consequentialist theories disagree about which outcomes matter. Predictions and who is affected become central questions.

An example

Comparing two policies by the harm each prevents uses an outcome-focused lens.

Put it into practice

Identify whose outcomes are counted and what is uncertain.
