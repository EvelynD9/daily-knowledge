Ideas travel and change

Cultural diffusion is the spread of practices, objects or ideas between groups.

The idea

Adoption can involve adaptation rather than exact copying. Contact, migration and communication create routes for exchange.

An example

A food tradition travels with migrants and develops new local versions.

Trace both the route and the changes.
