Order matters before explanation

Chronology establishes when events happened relative to one another.

The idea

A timeline can rule out impossible explanations: a cause must precede its effect. Sequence alone still does not prove causation.

An example

A policy introduced after a protest cannot explain why that protest began.

Put it into practice

Put the events in order before connecting them.
