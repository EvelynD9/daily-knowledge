An average can hide where people live

Population density divides population by land area.

The idea

A regional average does not reveal settlement patterns within it. People may be concentrated in a few cities.

An example

An area with 10,000 people across 100 km² has average density 100 people per km², even if most live in one town.

Put it into practice

Ask about distribution as well as the average.
