A chemical message needs a receiver

Hormones act on target cells with suitable receptors.

The idea

A hormone may circulate widely, but cells respond according to their receptors and signalling machinery.

An example

A signal in the blood does not make every tissue perform the same action.

Put it into practice

Ask which cells can receive the message.
