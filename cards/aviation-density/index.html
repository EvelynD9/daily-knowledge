Hotter air can change performance

Density altitude expresses air density as an equivalent altitude in a standard atmosphere.

The idea

Higher density altitude means less dense air. This can reduce aircraft performance; the effect depends on the aircraft and conditions.

An example

A hot day can make an airport perform like a higher-elevation location than its actual elevation suggests.

Put it into practice

Distinguish airport elevation from the density of the air.
