One cold day does not describe a climate

Weather is short-term atmospheric conditions; climate describes long-term patterns.

The idea

Individual days vary around broader distributions. A trend requires observations over longer periods.

An example

A rainy holiday tells you about those days, not the destination's entire climate.

Match the timescale of the evidence to the question.
