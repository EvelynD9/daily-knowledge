Objects carry shared meanings

Material culture includes the physical objects people create and use.

The idea

Nonmaterial culture includes ideas and values. The two interact: an object can embody a belief or social relationship.

An example

A wedding ring is a physical object linked to ideas about commitment.

Put it into practice

Ask both what an object does and what it represents.
