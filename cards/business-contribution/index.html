A sale has to help pay the fixed costs

Contribution per unit is selling price minus variable cost per unit.

The idea

That remainder contributes toward fixed costs and then profit. It is not automatically the final profit on the sale.

An example

A €10 item with €6 variable cost contributes €4 before fixed costs are covered.

Put it into practice

Calculate what one extra sale actually contributes.
