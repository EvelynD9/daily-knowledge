Sleep is part of learning

Learning does not end when practice stops; sleep helps stabilise and reorganise new memories.

The idea

Different sleep stages support aspects of memory, emotional processing, and skill refinement. Cutting sleep can weaken both attention during learning and later recall.

An example

Reviewing an idea briefly before sleep and recalling it the next day can be more effective than extending a tired late-night session.

Put it into practice

Treat sleep as the final step of study, not as time stolen from it.
