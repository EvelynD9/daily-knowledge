The slowest stage can set the pace

A bottleneck constrains the throughput of a process.

The idea

Speeding up a stage that already has spare capacity may not increase final output. The limiting stage deserves attention.

An example

A café can prepare 30 orders an hour but take payment for only 15; faster preparation alone will not fix the queue.

Put it into practice

Find the limiting step before improving the busiest-looking step.
