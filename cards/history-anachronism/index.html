Do not lend the past tomorrow's knowledge

An anachronism places something in the wrong historical time.

The idea

Using later technology or information to explain earlier decisions can distort what people could actually know or do.

An example

Judging an old voyage as if its crew had satellite forecasts assigns them tools they never possessed.

Put it into practice

Separate knowledge available then from knowledge available now.
