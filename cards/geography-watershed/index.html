A river connects a whole landscape

A watershed is the area that drains toward a shared outlet.

The idea

Water and material from upstream can affect places downstream. Watershed boundaries often follow high ground, not political borders.

An example

Rain on different sides of a ridge may flow into different rivers.

Put it into practice

Trace the drainage area, not just the visible river.
