Break-even asks how many sales cover the basics

Break-even occurs when total revenue equals total costs.

The idea

In a simple single-product model, fixed costs divided by contribution per unit gives the units needed to break even.

An example

With fixed costs of €100 and €5 contribution per sale, 20 sales cover those fixed costs.
