Build a mind you’re proud of.

Remembering is a form of practice

Trying to recall an idea can strengthen later memory.

The idea

Rereading makes material familiar. Retrieval asks you to reconstruct it without looking; checking afterward helps correct mistakes.

An example

Close a card, explain it in one sentence, then compare your explanation with the original.

Put it into practice

Test your memory before reaching for the answer.
