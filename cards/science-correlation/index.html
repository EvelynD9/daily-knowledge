Correlation is a clue, not a cause

Two things can move together without one producing the other.

The idea

A third variable may influence both, the direction may be reversed, or the pattern may be coincidence. Causation needs a mechanism and stronger evidence.

An example

Ice-cream sales and sunburns rise together because hot, sunny weather increases both—not because ice cream causes sunburn.

Put it into practice

When you see a correlation, search for hidden variables and ask what experiment could separate the explanations.
