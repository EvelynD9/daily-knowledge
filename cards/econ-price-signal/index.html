Prices are compressed information

A price does more than state cost: it coordinates what buyers want with what sellers can supply.

The idea

When demand rises or supply falls, prices often increase. That signal encourages conservation by buyers and greater production by sellers.

An example

A sudden rise in hotel prices during an event signals scarce rooms, even if you know nothing about local occupancy data.

Put it into practice

When a price moves sharply, look for the change in demand, supply, expectations, or all three.
