Understanding starts with local meaning

Cultural relativism examines practices within their cultural context.

The idea

Understanding a practice is different from endorsing everything about it. Context helps explain what people mean and value.

An example

Before judging an unfamiliar ceremony, ask participants what its actions signify.

Put it into practice

Seek explanation before assuming the meaning.
