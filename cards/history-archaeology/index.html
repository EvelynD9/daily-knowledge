An object's location is part of its story

Archaeological context links a find to its position and associated material.

The idea

An object removed without recording its surroundings loses evidence about use, date and relationships.

An example

A coin found in a documented floor layer says more than a coin with no recorded origin.

Put it into practice

Ask where a find came from, not only what it is.
