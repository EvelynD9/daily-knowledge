Safety improves when resources work together

Resource management coordinates people, information and available tools.

The idea

Clear communication and decisions help a team use knowledge distributed across its members and supporting services.

An example

A team member voices a concern early, allowing the others to check an assumption before it becomes a problem.

Put it into practice

Make concerns clear and invite useful information.
