Repeated actions can express belonging

Rituals are patterned actions with shared symbolic meaning.

The idea

A ritual can mark a transition or connect participants to a group. Repetition alone does not explain its meaning.

An example

A graduation ceremony marks a change in status through familiar gestures and symbols.

Put it into practice

Ask what the action marks and who recognises its meaning.
