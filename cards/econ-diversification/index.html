Diversification protects against being precisely wrong

Spreading exposure reduces the damage caused by any one asset, sector, or outcome.

The idea

Diversification cannot remove market-wide risk, but it limits risks specific to a single company or idea. Its value comes from assets not moving identically.

An example

Owning ten similar technology stocks may feel diversified, yet they can respond to the same interest-rate shock.

Put it into practice

Count independent sources of risk, not just the number of things you own.
