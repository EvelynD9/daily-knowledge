Build a mind you’re proud of.

Your favourite explanation needs a challenger

Confirmation bias favours evidence that supports an existing belief.

The idea

Searching only for agreement can make a weak belief feel well supported. Contrary evidence deserves a fair test too.

An example

You believe a product is excellent and read only positive reviews, ignoring recurring complaints.

Put it into practice

Ask what evidence would change your mind.
