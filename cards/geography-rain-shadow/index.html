A mountain can divide wet and dry

A rain shadow can form on the downwind side of a mountain range.

The idea

Air rising over mountains cools and may lose moisture as precipitation. Descending air on the other side can be warmer and drier.

An example

Two nearby valleys can have different rainfall because a mountain separates their airflow.

Put it into practice

Consider prevailing wind and terrain together.
