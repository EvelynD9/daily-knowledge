Build a mind you’re proud of.

Small sessions can outlast one marathon

Spaced practice distributes learning across time.

The idea

Returning after a gap can support lasting memory. Useful spacing depends on the material and how long you want to remember it.

An example

Revisit an idea tomorrow and later in the week instead of reading it five times immediately.

Put it into practice

Leave a gap, then recall again.
