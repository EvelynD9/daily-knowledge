Agreement is stronger when sources are independent

Corroboration compares evidence from more than one source.

The idea

Two accounts repeating one earlier report are not two independent witnesses. Differences can be informative as well as agreement.

An example

Compare a diary, a dated photograph and an official record rather than three copies of one article.

Put it into practice

Trace where each account obtained its information.
