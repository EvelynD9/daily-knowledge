Moving through air has a cost

Drag is an aerodynamic force opposing motion relative to the air.

The idea

Shape, speed and flow conditions affect drag. Drag associated with producing lift is one part of the total.

An example

Changing an aircraft's configuration can change drag even when the aircraft stays the same size.

Put it into practice

Ask which source of drag changes in the situation.
