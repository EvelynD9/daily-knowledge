Movement needs a connection

Tendons transmit force from muscle to bone.

The idea

Muscles generate tension; connective tissue transfers it to the skeleton. Tendons and ligaments serve different main connections.

An example

A contracting calf muscle pulls through the Achilles tendon to move the heel.

Put it into practice

Distinguish muscle-to-bone from bone-to-bone connections.
