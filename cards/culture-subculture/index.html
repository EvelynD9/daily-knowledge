One society contains many cultural worlds

A subculture shares distinctive practices within a larger society.

The idea

Membership does not mean rejecting every wider social value. People can belong to several groups at once.

An example

A professional community develops vocabulary and routines that outsiders do not immediately recognise.

Put it into practice

Avoid treating a whole society as culturally identical.
