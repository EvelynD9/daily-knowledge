Age can become part of beauty

Patina is the visible change a material develops through time, exposure, and use.

The idea

Cracks, oxidation, fading, and wear can carry evidence of history. Conservation often balances original appearance with respect for that history.

An example

The green surface of old bronze is not paint; it is a chemical transformation that has become part of how the object is recognised.

Put it into practice

Before calling wear a flaw, ask whether it destroys the object—or records its life.
