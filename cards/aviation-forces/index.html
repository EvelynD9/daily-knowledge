Flight is a balance of forces

Lift, weight, thrust and drag act on an aircraft.

The idea

Lift and drag are defined relative to airflow; weight acts toward Earth. A steady straight flight can have balanced forces without being stationary.

An example

An aircraft cruising at constant velocity still has forces acting on it.

Put it into practice

Distinguish balanced forces from an absence of forces.
