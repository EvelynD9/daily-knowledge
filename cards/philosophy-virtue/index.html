Build a mind you’re proud of.

Ethics can ask who you are becoming

Virtue ethics emphasises character and cultivated qualities.

The idea

Instead of examining only rules or outcomes, it asks how judgement and habits express qualities such as honesty and courage.

An example

Practising honest feedback builds a disposition, not just a record of isolated compliant acts.

Put it into practice

What kind of character does this repeated action cultivate?
