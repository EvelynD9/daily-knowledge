Another ethical lens asks about duties

Deontological ethics gives duties or constraints a central role.

The idea

An attractive outcome does not automatically justify every method. Different theories explain duties and conflicts differently.

An example

Refusing to deceive a customer despite a possible sale can reflect a duty-based judgement.

Put it into practice

Ask which principle the action respects or violates.
