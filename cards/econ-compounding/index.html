Compounding rewards consistency

Returns can generate their own returns, turning time into a financial force multiplier.

The idea

With compounding, growth is calculated on the original amount and on past growth. The curve looks slow early on, then accelerates as the base becomes larger.

An example

At a hypothetical 7% annual return, €100 becomes about €197 after 10 years and about €387 after 20—without adding more money.

Put it into practice

A sustainable habit started early often matters more than waiting for the perfect moment or product.
