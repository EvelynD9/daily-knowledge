Bone is living tissue

Bone is continually maintained through formation and resorption.

The idea

Remodelling renews tissue and responds to mechanical demands and biological signals. Bone is not an inert support beam.

An example

The skeleton keeps changing even after a person has reached adult height.

Put it into practice

Think of bone as an active tissue, not a fixed object.
