Breathing creates an exchange surface

Oxygen and carbon dioxide diffuse across the thin alveolar-capillary barrier.

The idea

Differences in partial pressure drive gases in opposite directions between air and blood.

An example

Oxygen passes from alveolar air into blood while carbon dioxide moves from blood toward the air to be exhaled.

Put it into practice

Separate moving air from exchanging gases.
