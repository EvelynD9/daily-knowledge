Privacy and your choices

One Card Wiser works without an account. This version keeps your learning data in your browser.

Progress on this device

Your language preference, topic choices, completed cards, dates, review dates, quiz answers, saved ideas and source previews are stored in localStorage. Clearing this site’s browser data removes that progress. There is no cross-device synchronisation in this version.

Offline installation

A service worker caches the app’s public files so written cards can open offline after a successful first visit. It does not upload your saved learning history.

External services

GitHub Pages hosts this website and receives normal web requests. Exploring a Wikipedia source preview sends a request from your device to Wikimedia, which receives connection information such as your IP address. Source links open the named third-party website. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Analytics

The learning app uses optional Google Analytics only after you choose Allow analytics. If you allow it, app visits and actions such as card completion, quiz answers, review completion, saves and shares may be measured with their topic. Public card, topic and guide pages do not load Analytics in this version. Your saved-card list and local learning history are not uploaded by this feature. Google may receive usage and device information.

You can open Analytics choices in the app, decline optional analytics or withdraw permission there. Withdrawal disables future analytics events and removes Google Analytics cookies accessible to this website. Previously collected data is not automatically deleted by that choice.

Sharing

Sharing sends only the idea and public link you choose to the destination you select. Story downloads are created on your device.

Feedback reports

Feedback is prepared on your device and is not sent automatically. If you open the GitHub feedback link, your message, selected category, interface language, current card identifier (when applicable) and app version are passed to GitHub in the link. Submitting requires a GitHub account and creates a public issue. Do not include personal information. Your saved cards, quiz answers and learning history are not included.
