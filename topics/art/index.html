Empty space is an active choice

Negative space is the area around and between subjects—and it shapes what you notice first.

Light can build drama

Chiaroscuro uses strong contrast between light and dark to model form and direct emotion.

Opposite colours intensify each other

Complementary colours sit opposite on a colour wheel; blue and orange are a pair on a traditional artists’ wheel.

Perspective is a designed illusion

Linear perspective makes parallel lines appear to converge, creating depth on a flat surface.

Composition controls the journey of your eye

Composition is the arrangement of visual elements—and it determines how an image unfolds over time.

Context can change an object into art

Meaning depends not only on what an object is, but on where, when, and why it is presented.

Age can become part of beauty

Patina is the visible change a material develops through time, exposure, and use.

Abstraction is not the absence of skill

Abstract art reduces, reshapes, or abandons visible reality to focus on relationships such as colour, rhythm, and form.
