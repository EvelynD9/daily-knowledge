Social norms are invisible instructions

Norms are shared expectations about behaviour that people often follow without a written rule.

Familiar is not automatically universal

Ethnocentrism judges another culture mainly by one's own cultural standards.

Understanding starts with local meaning

Cultural relativism examines practices within their cultural context.

Objects carry shared meanings

Material culture includes the physical objects people create and use.

Values and norms play different roles

Values express what a group considers important; norms guide behaviour.

One society contains many cultural worlds

A subculture shares distinctive practices within a larger society.

Ideas travel and change

Cultural diffusion is the spread of practices, objects or ideas between groups.

Repeated actions can express belonging

Rituals are patterned actions with shared symbolic meaning.
