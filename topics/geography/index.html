Every map makes an argument

A map is a selective model of the world, not a neutral copy of it.

The surface is moving beneath us

Plate tectonics describes motion of Earth's lithospheric plates.

A river connects a whole landscape

A watershed is the area that drains toward a shared outlet.

A city can hold extra heat

Urban heat islands are urban areas warmer than nearby surroundings.

One cold day does not describe a climate

Weather is short-term atmospheric conditions; climate describes long-term patterns.

Scale decides how much fits on a map

A map scale relates distance on the map to distance on the ground.

A rain shadow can form on the downwind side of a mountain range.

An average can hide where people live

Population density divides population by land area.
